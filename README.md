# Travlioz — Multimodal Travel Platform

**Discover destinations. Plan journeys. Explore experiences.**

Travlioz is a multimodal travel platform designed to make travel discovery and planning more intuitive, personalized, and convenient. It brings travel information, destination exploration, and activity discovery together in one place, helping travelers move from inspiration to planning and booking.

## ✨ Overview

Planning a trip often involves switching between multiple platforms to research destinations, compare experiences, organize travel details, and find booking options. Travlioz aims to simplify this process through a unified travel experience that combines different ways of discovering and interacting with travel information.

The platform's multimodal approach is intended to make travel planning more flexible by bringing together information in formats suited to different traveler needs.

## 🚀 Core Vision

- **Destination Discovery:** Explore destinations, attractions, and places of interest.
- **Activity Exploration:** Discover activities, tours, and experiences available at a destination.
- **Multimodal Interaction:** Support richer travel discovery through complementary forms of information, such as text, visual content, and location-based context, as supported by the application.
- **Personalized Travel Planning:** Help travelers explore options relevant to their destinations, dates, and preferences.
- **Activity Booking Navigation:** Connect travelers with activity providers so they can review available experiences and continue to the relevant booking page.
- **Unified Travel Experience:** Bring essential stages of travel discovery and planning into a single platform.

## 🧭 How It Works

1. **Discover** — Start with a destination or travel interest.
2. **Explore** — Review relevant attractions, activities, and experiences.
3. **Compare** — Evaluate available options and their details.
4. **Plan** — Narrow down experiences based on travel requirements.
5. **Book** — Open the relevant provider's booking page to continue the reservation process.

## 🎯 Goals

Travlioz aims to:

- Reduce the effort involved in researching travel experiences.
- Make destination and activity discovery easier to navigate.
- Provide a more connected journey from exploration to booking.
- Support a flexible and accessible travel-planning experience.
- Create a foundation for richer, multimodal travel assistance.
